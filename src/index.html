01 / WHO WE ARE

SCIENCE
WITH PURPOSE.

Meridian Biomedical Solution is a UAE-based healthcare technology and biomedical solutions company supporting hospitals, clinics, laboratories, and healthcare facilities with reliable equipment and professional technical services.

From medical equipment maintenance, calibration, and installation to clinic setup and troubleshooting, we provide practical solutions that keep healthcare operations safe and efficient. Through Loggfi, our healthcare monitoring and data-logging solution, we also support temperature monitoring and compliance in critical environments.

Our goal is simple: to be a trusted technical partner throughout the healthcare equipment lifecycle.
